Research

I lead the data pipeline team in Dr. Gu's Gangliaguardian Lab at UT Dallas, studying ultrasonic vocalizations (USVs) in autism-model mice. My work bridges computational bioacoustics, signal processing, and statistical linguistics — applying FFT-based spectral feature extraction, NFFT parameter tuning for spectrogram resolution, and linguistic laws originally derived from human language to understand how mice communicate. The goal: find structure in the squeaks that tells us something real about the neuroscience of autism.
